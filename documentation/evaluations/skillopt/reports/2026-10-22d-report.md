## Insertion anchors land before the heading

`composeBaselineInsertion` (`baseline-insertion.ts`) splices the paragraph in **immediately before** the `headingAnchor` line. The paragraph therefore ends the *previous* section; it does not land in the section the anchor names. As a result, every 10-22c candidate text sat one section earlier than the 10-22c report describes:

- U2 sat at the end of "Working in a git worktree or another directory", not after the intent paragraph in "Reading a hook snippet".
- C1's three paragraphs sat at the ends of:
  - "Requirements signoff" (the impact-review paragraph);
  - "Impact reviews" (the merge paragraph);
  - "Merge commits and migrations" (the conflict paragraph).

The optimizer's own `revise-1a` output for usage made the same mistake: its objective asked for "the end of Reading a hook snippet" and it anchored on `## Reading a hook snippet`.

Other limits of the insertion mechanism:

- Two insertions may not share an anchor (`insertion_anchor_duplicate`).
- The last section of a skill cannot receive an insertion.
- The phrase "step 3" is refused as `metadata_leak` by the `step[-_ ]…\d` pattern.

This is a usability defect in the composition contract or its documentation, not a scoring defect. The new candidates in this run are anchored on the heading that *follows* their intended section.

## Remaining gaps

- **Single-family vetoes on noisy families.** All three runs so far (10-22c, and this one twice per skill) were stopped by one family losing one or two cells while the overall mean rose. These families have 3–6 cells per arm, and the baseline itself swings by up to 25 mean points between cohorts. A pooled multi-cohort rule, or larger per-family cohorts, would be needed to tell candidate effects from noise. Either one is a policy decision.
- **usage fact-predicate-modeling `predicate-args`:** a candidate could scope U3's `kb_model` requirement step to strict and typed-rule claims only, and say outright that predicate-lane claims skip it. Not run.
- **commit-review label symbol:** guidance to pick the requirement that governs the new symbol, not the file's lead requirement, is untested.
- **Insertion-anchor semantics:** the contract or the optimizer prompt should say that a paragraph lands before the anchor heading.

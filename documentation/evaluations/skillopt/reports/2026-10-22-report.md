# Kibi SkillOpt campaign — 2026-10-22

## Outcome

**kibi-usage** is confirmed with no regression. Over 30 cells per arm it went from 85.5 to 88, and hard passes went from 22/30 to 24/30. The gain is in intent-consult, which rose from 47.5 to 60. Safe-mutation-direction, which never hard-passed before, now scores 100 in every cell of both arms on the seeded fixture.

**kibi-decisions** and **kibi-commit-review** were not evaluated. The campaign cannot freeze any candidate for either skill, because their own canonical bodies fail the candidate-body content policy. No target cells were spent on them.

Canonical skills are unchanged. The packaged usage file is a review proposal, and adopting it needs explicit approval of its hash.

Host: Claude Code CLI 2.1.308 (`KIBI_SKILLOPT_HOST=claude-code`). Optimizer **claude-opus-5-5 / high**; target **claude-haiku-5-5 / low**. The target cap was 400 episodes, of which 150 were used. This source adds SkillOpt families for kibi-decisions and kibi-commit-review, dry-run/commit parity for `kb_upsert`, and a seeded safe-mutation-direction fixture for kibi-usage. The usage baseline body is `a66c75c6…`; it differs from the 2026-10-21b baseline.

Rounds (kibi-usage only):

1. **Smoke**: 1 repeat, baseline plus one composed candidate, 10 cells. The candidate was the unconfirmed 2026-10-21b intent-consult paragraph, re-anchored on the new baseline.
2. **Round 1**: the smoke candidate plus two optimizer candidates, 2 repeats, 40 cells. The optimizer candidates came from public feedback on intent-consult (one scoped to edits under "Safe workflow", one stating a general discovery order under "Interface Selection").
3. **Round 2**: the best round-1 candidate, a second optimizer candidate, and a host composition of the round-1 winner plus one scope sentence, 2 repeats, 40 cells. The second optimizer candidate and the scope sentence both came from feedback that the round-1 paragraphs coincided with modeling cells that stopped before writing.
4. **Final evaluation**: the selected candidate alone against the baseline on a fresh fixture root, 3 repeats, 15 cells per arm.
5. **Confirmation**: 3 more repeats on a fresh cohort, combined with the final cohort to 30 cells per arm.

Scores, hard passes and worst family follow the definitions in the [2026-10-04 report](2026-10-04-report.md). A cell scores 60 for the final state with no critical failure, 25 for the required call order and 15 for isolation. This is a small repeated development comparison. There was no held-out run, and no significance is claimed.

## Harness issue: decisions and commit-review candidates cannot be frozen

Every candidate path validates the complete composed body with `validateCandidateBody` (`scripts/skillopt-eval/variants.ts`). The insertion path does this at `baseline-insertion.ts` line 249. The body-replacement path does it in `replaceCampaignBody`, and manifest loading does it again. Both canonical bodies already fail that policy, so any candidate inherits the failure:

| Skill | Canonical body line | Rejection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| kibi-decisions | "When this applies": "Codex, Cursor and ZCode tell you to ask first …" | `candidate_prohibited_host_or_provider_claim` (the host/provider pattern matches `Cursor`) |
| kibi-commit-review | Steps, item 4: "`.kb/symbol-coordinates.yaml` that belong to this change (use `git add -p` …" | `candidate_direct_kb_guidance` (a `.kb` path plus `use` with no negation on the same line) |

Reproduction, offline and at zero cost:

- `compose --body-file` with the unchanged canonical body fails with the codes above. For kibi-usage the same check returns `replacement_unchanged`, as expected.
- `compose --insertion-file` with a harmless one-sentence paragraph ("## Report" for decisions, "## Steps" for commit-review) fails with the same codes.

`revise` composes the optimizer paragraph through the same function, so it would spend an optimizer call and then fail. It was not run. `evaluate` needs at least one candidate manifest, so the new families' fixtures could not be exercised live either. Both skills were stopped at 0 target cells, and no code was changed.

Possible fixes, for review: apply the content policy only to the inserted paragraph or to the changed region of a body replacement, or reword the two canonical lines. The decisions line names hosts on purpose, so it may need an exemption rather than a rewording.

What the feedback found:

- **intent-consult** is the baseline's weak family in every cohort (27.5–51.67). Either it skips the `kb_query` read of the governing requirement after `kb_search` (first missing call `kb_query`), or it makes the read and loses `workflow-signal-2`, one answer signal. Both round-1 paragraphs that called the search answer layer discovery only lifted the family to 100 (2/2).
- **fact-predicate-modeling**: with those paragraphs, one cell in two stopped after `kb_model` predicates without writing (first missing call `kb_upsert`; score 15, all lane signals missing). The general discovery-order paragraph, which put `kb_model` behind the exact read, did not have this problem but did not fix intent-consult.
- The edit-scoped paragraph swung from 91.5 to 65 between cohorts. In round 2 both its modeling cells stopped before writing, and one validation-recovery cell changed the wrong thing.
- The round-2 optimizer candidate kept modeling at 100 but lost both validation-recovery cells (40 and 15).
- Adding one sentence to the edit-scoped paragraph — the rule adds nothing to modeling or write tasks, which still end with the real `kb_upsert` and the closing `kb_check` — removed the stop-before-write cells. The remaining modeling loss was one `predicate-args` cell (40), a failure the baseline also shows. This candidate was selected.

### Final evaluation and confirmation

The candidate is one paragraph under "Safe workflow". It says: before editing a tracked source file, `kb_search` with `sourceLocations` is discovery only; `kb_query` by id must exact-read each current governing requirement before the edit; check, status and coverage do not replace that read. It also says the rule adds nothing to a modeling or write task, which still ends with the real `kb_upsert` and the closing `kb_check`.

The final cohort alone went from 86.33 (11/15) to 80 (10/15), no regression **false** (mean, hard passes, fact-predicate-modeling, intent-consult). In that cohort the candidate's intent-consult cells all completed the call order but lost `workflow-signal-2` (40, 3/3). The confirmation cohort alone went from 84.67 to 96: intent-consult 2/3 at 100 and fact-predicate-modeling 3/3 at 100. The tables combine both cohorts.

## Remaining gaps

- **Harness**: kibi-decisions and kibi-commit-review cannot produce a candidate until the body-policy issue above is resolved. Their new families are still unexercised live.
- **usage intent-consult**: with the candidate the call order is complete in every cell, but 4/6 cells still lose `workflow-signal-2`. The baseline loses the same signal in 4/6 cells, and in 3/6 it also skips `kb_query`. The paragraph fixes the read; it does not fix the answer signal.
- **usage fact-predicate-modeling**: one cell in six per arm scores 40 on `predicate-args` (wrong predicate arguments) with a complete call order. Paragraphs about discovery order can make agents stop after `kb_model` without writing. The scope sentence prevented that here, so it should stay with any rewording.
- **usage validation-recovery**: one cell in six per arm loses `final-validation-recovery` and `workflow-outcome` with a complete call order. This is the same pattern seen in 2026-10-21b; it is not specific to the candidate.

# Kibi SkillOpt campaign — 2026-10-22b

## Outcome

**kibi-commit-review** is confirmed with no regression. Over 30 cells per arm it went from 44.67 to 72.67, and hard passes went from 7/30 to 16/30. Security failures went from 8 to 0: every baseline security failure was a direct file read under `.kb/`. The largest gains are in coordinate-refresh-only (46.67 to 100) and staged-impact-missing (46.67 to 90).

**kibi-decisions** is not confirmed. The selected candidate fixes the compile-intent-review closeout, but the final cohort lost on mean (76.67 to 71.67) and hard passes (9/15 to 7/15). No confirmation was run and nothing was packaged.

This is the first live evaluation of both skills. All ten new families ran live with working setups. No cell failed on infrastructure, and every family reached 100 in at least one cell.

Canonical skills are unchanged. The packaged commit-review file is a review proposal, and adopting it needs explicit approval of its hash.

## Scope and method

Host: Claude Code CLI 2.1.308 (`KIBI_SKILLOPT_HOST=claude-code`). Optimizer **claude-opus-5-5 / high**; target **claude-haiku-5-5 / low**. The target cap was 250 cells, of which 201 were used. Source tree: develop plus `ef9b95dce`, which makes the candidate content policy judge only the lines a candidate adds. It was already built and was clean for the whole run (every command recorded 0 dirty tracked files before and after).

That fix removes the blocker from the 2026-10-22 run: both canonical bodies now compose. A harmless one-sentence insertion froze offline for each skill.

Rounds, per skill:

1. **Smoke**: 1 repeat, baseline plus a harmless composed candidate (one sentence about keeping the report short), 10 cells.
2. **Round 1**: one optimizer candidate from an objective plus public feedback, and one host-composed paragraph, 2 repeats, 30 cells planned. This round was interrupted; see "Interrupted round 1".
3. **Round 2**: the round-1 optimizer candidate plus one new host composition, 2 repeats, 30 cells.
4. **Final evaluation**: the selected candidate alone against the baseline, 3 repeats, 15 cells per arm.
5. **Confirmation** (commit-review only): 3 more repeats on a fresh cohort, combined with the final cohort to 30 cells per arm.

Scores, hard passes and worst family follow the definitions in the [2026-10-04 report](2026-10-04-report.md). This is a small repeated development comparison. There was no held-out run, and no significance is claimed.

## Harness and fixture observations

- **No fixture or harness defect was found.** Every family passed live in at least one cell: 100 with a hard pass in some cell of some arm.
- **compile-intent-review baseline loss is behavioral.** In both smoke arms and most later baseline cells, the agent compiled twice, judged the seeded rule `no_conflict`, applied the plan and made the new requirement firm with the person's words. The closing `kb_check` then still reported `must-priority-coverage` on the new requirement (it had no scenario), and the agent reported done. This lost the final-state and outcome signals. In 11 of the 14 compile-intent-review cells that applied a plan, the first `kb_apply_plan` was refused with `planHash does not match the canonical plan body` and the retry succeeded. No arm lost score from that refusal, but it costs a call in most cells.
- **commit-review direct reads.** Without guidance, the target opens `.kb/symbols.yaml` and `.kb/symbol-coordinates.yaml` with the file-read tool after the quoted gate output names them. Each such read is a security failure and scores 0. This happened in 8 of 30 baseline cells in final plus confirmation, and in 0 of 30 candidate cells.
- **Interrupted round 1 (operator issue, not product code).** The shell wrapper around both round-1 `evaluate` commands was stopped by the 30-minute limit on background tasks. Decisions had 15/30 cells done and commit-review 16/30. `evaluate` cannot resume: the roots stay at `status: running` with their reserved budget counted. The **31 cells** were spent without a complete evaluation. Their recorded per-cell scores (`campaign-state.json`) were used for selection only. Later stages ran with a 2-hour limit.

## Remaining gaps

- **commit-review impact-review-preparation**: 4/6 candidate cells and 3/6 non-security baseline cells score 40. They ran `kb_prepare_impact_review` but lost two signals: the drafted record does not keep the prepared headers or file set, and the `src/fixture.ts` decision does not cite the seeded rule as `still_current`. The candidate does not address this.
- **commit-review staged-rule-conflict**: the candidate is at 1/6 hard passes. Three cells report no conflict in the answer (workflow-signal-3), and in 4/6 the agent stops after `kb_search` without `kb_query`.
- **commit-review merge-commit-scope**: 4/6 per arm lose workflow-signal-4, because the answer does not name the file taken unchanged from the merged branch. One report sentence fixed this in 1 of 2 cells but cost staged-impact-missing cells. It was not kept.
- **decisions firm-rule-conflict**: 2/3 cells per arm in the final cohort lose workflow-signal-3, because the answer does not report the conflict as the verdict or conflict field. A paragraph stating that answer shape (round 2) coincided with the agent skipping the source-linked query.
- **decisions write tasks**: paragraphs about closing write flows sometimes make the agent stop after dry runs and `kb_model` without the real `kb_upsert`. This happened in stated-rule-capture with both the harmless and the selected candidate.

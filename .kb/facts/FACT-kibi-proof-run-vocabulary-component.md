The words proof runs and contracts use: result outcomes such as passed, failed and timed_out, failure phases setup, collection, execution, teardown and infrastructure, and receipt and coverage page limits. It is vocabulary, not evaluation, and it is not kibi.proofs.vocabulary, which names the proof kinds and claim binding verdicts rather than how one run went.

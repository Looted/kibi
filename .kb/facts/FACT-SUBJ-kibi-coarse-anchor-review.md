The kibi check review of requirements whose only implementation evidence is a whole-file anchor on source that an extractor can split into smaller units such as functions. Files no extractor covers do not count, and JSON anchors do. It is not the mixed-purpose symbol diagnostic, which looks at symbols rather than anchors.

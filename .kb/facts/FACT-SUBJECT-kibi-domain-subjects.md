The domain subject layer of a Kibi knowledge base: subject facts that define the things a product is about, how subjects relate (dotted keys, part_of, kind_of, distinct_from), and the anchors that tie each unmodeled requirement claim to the subjects it is about, so requirements on one subject are read together. It is not the modeling of claims as property or predicate facts.

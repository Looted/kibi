A recorded reason in a kb_search answer that comes from the governing requirement itself must say who it came from and on what date, read from the requirement's origin, when the origin records them.

## Why

A reason reads differently when a person gave it than when an agent guessed it. The origin already records who approved a requirement and when, but the search answer quoted the reason without it, so the agent could not tell a maintainer's rule from an earlier agent's note.

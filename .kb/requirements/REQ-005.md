Relationships are first-class: `implements`, `validates`, `specified_by`,
`covered_by`, `depends_on`, `relates_to`, `constrained_by`, etc.
Each relationship triple is stored in RDF with a provenance timestamp.

## Why
Traceability needs to say how two things relate, not only that they do. A symbol implementing a requirement means something different from a test validating it. Timestamps let a reader see when a link was made. Inferred from: README "E2E behavior is traceable"; ADR-010.

Kibi must keep deterministic inference capabilities available for product and
automation use, but they are not exposed as a raw public inference surface.

The public MCP interface remains curated: exact lookup, curated discovery/reporting,
mutation, and validation are allowed, while arbitrary inference predicates remain
internal.

## Why
This was an implementation-time choice, not a product decision: exposing raw inference predicates would make the public surface unbounded and hard to keep stable, so agents get curated answers while inference stays inside the product. It stands only as long as it produces the richest KB. If curated answers ever stop agents from recording or checking something they need, revisit it. Inferred from: ADR "Discovery tools…" for the original choice; maintainer, 2026-11-11, for the condition.

Kibi must list each requirement once per preparation, with its before and after fingerprints, instead of repeating its entity for each file.

## Why

A requirement that several files implement was repeated whole for each of them, about 2,300 characters each time; the decision binds to its fingerprints, which one entry carries.

## Context

requirementBindings lists the requirements the page's files name, each with its before and after state, entity fingerprint and artifact path; a file row names its requirement ids.

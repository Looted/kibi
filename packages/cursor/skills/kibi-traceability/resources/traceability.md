# Traceability detail

Model one requirement clause at a time. Use strict fact lanes for contradiction
checks, predicate schemas for project ontology, and observation/meta facts for
bugs and workarounds. Query all relationship endpoints before writing; a graph
link is not proof until the scenario, test, symbol, and fresh receipt are
readable at the current snapshot.

Choose the owner by decision, not by proximity. For each symbol ask which
requirement's rule its body decides; link that one. Entry points, delegating
wrappers and forwarding adapters usually decide nothing and stay unlinked to
the behavior they pass through. A requirement that only whole-file anchors
implement (`coarse_anchor_only_review`) needs its deciding function linked.
A requirement at the edge of the review thresholds (8 symbols, 6 scenarios,
8 tests, 5 dependents) is a prompt to check whether it states one behavior,
never a quota to fill or to stay just under. The split and relink recipes are
in `kibi-usage/resources/kb-quality.md`.

The `kibi-usage` skill now limits the consultation before a source edit that keeps the governing rule to two calls: `kb_search` with the file, then one `kb_query`. Status, check and coverage run after the edit. For a modeling task, the skill spells out the full order: the exact `kb_query` of the target comes before any `kb_model` call, and the task does not stop at a dry run. In the 2026-10-22d SkillOpt campaign, over 42 cells per arm, the mean rose from 89.64 to 95.95 and hard passes from 33 to 38. Intent consultation went from 50 to 100. Two fact-predicate modeling cells lost an argument (100 to 80).

`kb_search` no longer ranks on a question's connecting words ("each", "other", "before", "after"), and it matches a past or third-person verb ending in -ied or -ies with its base form ("applied" with "apply", "queries" with "query"). Questions such as "what must a bootstrap plan guarantee before it is applied?" now reach the requirement that governs the topic instead of entities that only share the question's framing.
